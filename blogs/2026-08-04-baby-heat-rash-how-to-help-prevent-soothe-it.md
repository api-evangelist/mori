Warm weather means more opportunities to enjoy time outdoors with your little one, but it can also bring common skin concerns like baby heat rash. The good news is that heat rash is usually harmless and often improves once your baby has cooled down. Baby heat rash happens when sweat ducts become blocked, trapping sweat beneath the skin.
