Your baby’s first Christmas is one of those milestones that can feel exciting, emotional and a little overwhelming all at once. There are presents to buy, outfits to choose, family plans to make and plenty of Christmas traditions you might want to start. But your baby doesn't need a mountain of presents to make their first Christmas memorable.
